The Science of Wind-Down

Why 5-10 Minutes of Evening Journaling Calms Nighttime Cortisol

When you head to bed with a racing mind, your nervous system remains in high alert. Guided evening journaling acts as a gentle mental brake, signaling your adrenal glands that the day is safe to close.

01

Cognitive Closure

Putting lingering thoughts on paper stops your brain from looping endlessly in bed trying to remember tomorrow's tasks.

02

Vagus Nerve Relaxation

Unhurried, rhythmic handwriting activates the parasympathetic "rest-and-digest" branch, lowering heart rate and muscle tension.

03

Screen-Free Melatonin

Swapping bright screens for warm paper or an ambient dim reading light prevents blue light from suppressing your natural sleep hormone.
