Methodology & Standards

How Daily Health Review Audits Supplements

Our research desk adheres to four objective pillars before recommending any formulation.

1

Formula & Clinical Doses

We inspect the full label to ensure active ingredients match the exact therapeutic dosages verified in human clinical trials, avoiding hidden filler blends.

2

Ingredient Evidence

Every botanical extract is cross-referenced with peer-reviewed medical journals (e.g. PubMed, Am J Clin Nutr) to substantiate safety and biological efficacy.

3

cGMP Safety & Lab Purity

Formulations must be manufactured in US-based, FDA-registered, cGMP-certified facilities with third-party testing for zero microbial or heavy metal contamination.

4

Price & 100% Refund Terms

We verify the daily cost per serving against market competitors and confirm an unconditional 60 to 180-day money-back guarantee with hassle-free returns.
