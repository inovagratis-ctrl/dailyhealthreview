The "Miracle Molecule" That Keeps Arteries Wide and Supple

In 1998, three American scientists received the Nobel Prize in Medicine for discovering how a tiny signaling gas called Nitric Oxide tells your arteries to widen, relax, and pump life-giving oxygenated blood to every organ, muscle, and neuron in your body.

When you are in your 20s, your blood vessels produce abundant Nitric Oxide. Blood flows effortlessly, stamina remains high, and blood pressure stays within healthy target ranges.

The Age-Related Drop: By age 40, natural Nitric Oxide production drops by 50%. By age 70, you produce barely 25% of what you need. This causes arteries to stiffen, starving your brain, heart, and limbs of essential oxygen and micronutrients.
