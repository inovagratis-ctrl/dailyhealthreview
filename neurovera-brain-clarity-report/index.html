Inside NeuroVera: The 5 Core Botanical Ingredients

Nutraville formulated NeuroVera using five well-known herbal and fungal ingredients with long histories of traditional use and ongoing nutritional research:

1. Schisandra Fruit Extract (Schisandra Chinensis)

Traditionally used in Eastern herbal practices as an adaptogen, Schisandra is valued for helping the body adapt to daily environmental stressors. Nutritional researchers study its natural plant lignans for their antioxidant properties and potential role in supporting mental endurance.

2. Gotu Kola (Centella Asiatica)

Long recognized in traditional wellness systems, Gotu Kola is frequently studied for its triterpene compounds. It is commonly taken to support healthy circulation and promote calm, steady concentration during demanding daily tasks.

3. Purified Shilajit Extract

Sourced from mineral-rich mountainous regions, Shilajit contains natural fulvic acid along with a natural spectrum of trace minerals. In nutritional science, fulvic acid is appreciated for supporting cellular nutrient absorption and overall vitality.

4. Lion's Mane Mushroom (Hericium Erinaceus)

This distinctive mushroom has gained widespread attention in modern nootropic research for its natural bioactive compounds. It is widely used in nutritional protocols to support cognitive wellness, mental clarity, and focus.

5. Bacopa Monnieri

Bacopa is an ancient herb with a long history of traditional use for supporting memory recall and mental processing speed. Contemporary studies continue to evaluate how its natural bacoside compounds support cognitive performance over time.
