The Anatomy of Stiff Joints: Why Age, Repetitive Motion & Inflammation Rob Your Freedom

Healthy human joints are mechanical marvels. Inside every knee, hip, shoulder, and finger joint, a smooth layer of articular cartilage acts as a frictionless shock absorber, supported by a slippery, egg-white-like fluid called synovial fluid.

However, after age 40, three interconnected biological breakdowns begin:

🦴

1. Cartilage Erosion

Chondrocytes (cartilage cells) slow down collagen synthesis, leading to micro-cracks and thinning shock absorption.

💧

2. Synovial Dryness

Synovial lubricating fluid thins out, leading to tell-tale grinding, cracking, and bone-on-bone friction when climbing stairs.

🔥

3. Enzymatic Inflammation

5-LOX and COX-2 enzymes release inflammatory cytokines that swell the joint capsule, causing stubborn morning stiffness.

JointEternal was designed by NaturalCell to directly address all three pathways simultaneously, combining structural rebuilding blocks with natural botanical enzyme inhibitors.
