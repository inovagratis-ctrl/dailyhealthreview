The Mayo Clinic Insight: Is Your Brain Fog Actually "Type 3 Diabetes"?

For years, modern medicine treated memory loss and cognitive decline as an unavoidable genetic consequence of getting older. However, leading medical institutions including the Mayo Clinic have uncovered a startling biological reality: what many experience as "senior moments" is intimately linked to cerebral glucose toxicity.

The human brain consumes approximately 20% of the body's total glucose energy despite representing only 2% of total body mass. Over decades of consuming modern hidden sugars, glucose transporters across the blood-brain barrier become exhausted.

When excess sugar cannot be metabolized, it begins to crystallize around the synaptic junctions where brain cells communicate. Like sticky molasses poured into fine watch gears, this sugar buildup causes neuro-inflammation, impairs acetylcholine signaling, and starves neurons of life-giving oxygen.
