Step-by-Step Instructions

Claire's foolproof whipping technique for maximum airy lift.

Easy • 5 Steps
1

Preheat Oven & Separate Eggs

Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a flat baking sheet with unbleached parchment paper. Carefully separate the 3 eggs: place the whites in a completely grease-free mixing bowl, and the yolks into a food processor or small bowl.

2

Whip Egg Whites to Stiff Peaks

Add the 1/2 tsp of baking powder (or cream of tartar) to the egg whites. Using a hand mixer on high speed, whip for 3 to 4 minutes until glossy, cloud-like stiff peaks form that hold their shape when you lift the beaters.

3

Blend Cottage Cheese & Yolks

Blend the 1 cup of cottage cheese and the 3 egg yolks together until completely silky and velvety smooth with zero curds remaining.

4

Fold Gently & Shape Cloud Mounds

Using a silicone spatula, gently fold 1/3 of the whipped egg whites into the cottage cheese mixture to lighten it, then gently fold in the remaining whites with slow upward strokes to preserve all the airy volume. Spoon 4 generous cloud-like mounds onto your prepared baking sheet. Sprinkle toasted sesame or chia seeds over top if desired.

5

Bake to Golden Perfection & Serve Warm

Bake for 23 to 25 minutes until the tops are puffed, golden brown, and set. Turn off the oven and prop the door slightly open for 5 minutes so they settle gently without deflating. Serve warm with grass-fed butter, avocado, or your favorite clean toppings!
