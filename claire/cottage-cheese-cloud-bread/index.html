3-Ingredient Cottage Cheese Cloud Bread

Ingredients

Yield (Rolls) 4

20g of clean protein. Zero flour. Total lightness.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Claire's tested foolproof method for the ultimate cloud-like lift.

Easy • 5 Steps
1

Preheat Oven & Whip Egg Whites to Stiff Peaks

Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a flat baking sheet with parchment paper. Separate the 3 pasture-raised eggs into clean bowls. Add the 1/2 tsp of baking powder (or cream of tartar) to the egg whites. Using a hand mixer on high speed, whip for 3 minutes until glossy, stiff cloud peaks form.

Whipping 3 egg whites into stiff, airy cloud peaks

2

Blend Cottage Cheese & Egg Yolks

Place 1 cup of full-fat cottage cheese and the 3 egg yolks into your food processor. Blend on high speed for 45 to 60 seconds until the mixture transforms into a velvety, completely smooth cream with zero graininess or curd texture.

Claire blending cottage cheese & yolks silky smooth

3

Fold Gently to Preserve Airy Volume

Transfer the blended cottage cheese cream into a large bowl. Spoon 1/3 of the whipped egg whites in and gently fold using a silicone spatula to lighten the base. Then, gently fold in the remaining whites using slow, upward circular strokes to lock in all that delicate air.

Claire folding gently with spatula to keep maximum volume

4

Shape 4 Cloud Mounds on Baking Sheet

Using a large spoon, portion the fluffy batter into 4 equal cloud mounds on the lined baking sheet, leaving at least 2 inches between each mound. Lightly smooth the tops with the back of the spoon. Sprinkle toasted sesame seeds or seasoning over top if desired.

Claire shaping 4 generous cloud mounds on baking sheet

5

Bake to Golden Perfection & Serve Warm

Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 23 to 25 minutes until the tops turn puffed, lightly browned, and set to the touch. Turn off the oven, prop the door slightly open for 5 minutes, then serve warm with grass-fed butter, cream cheese, or avocado!

Warm, airy cloud bread served with grass-fed butter
