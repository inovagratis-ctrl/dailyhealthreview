Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Dissolve Himalayan Salt in Warm Water

Pour 1 cup (240ml) of comfortably warm filtered water into a clean glass. Add 1/4 teaspoon (1.5g) of pink Himalayan or Celtic sea salt. Stir for 10 seconds until the mineral crystals dissolve completely, creating an alkalizing, remineralizing base that neutralizes mouth acids.

Alkalizing warm mineral water base

2

Bruise & Steep Fresh Spearmint Leaves

Take 4 to 5 fresh organic spearmint leaves. Clap them between your palms or bruise them gently with your fingers to release the natural botanical polyphenols and essential menthol oils. Drop them into the warm water and let them infuse for 2 minutes.

Steeping fresh botanical spearmint

3

Add Cold-Pressed Virgin Coconut Oil

Add 1/2 teaspoon (2.5ml) of virgin unrefined coconut oil. The warm liquid will immediately melt the oil into tiny droplets. Coconut oil contains powerful medium-chain fatty acids (lauric acid) that naturally bind to yellowing plaque without stripping the good bacteria.

Swirling in cold-pressed virgin coconut oil

4

Swish for 60 Seconds & Spit Out

Take a generous mouthful and swish gently between your teeth and along your gumline for 60 seconds. Feel the cooling spearmint soothe your gums. Spit completely into the sink (do not swallow, as it has lifted trapped debris). Lightly rinse your mouth with plain warm water.

60-second gentle oral swishing & spit

5

Chew 1 Oral Probiotic Tablet to Repopulate

Immediately following your rinse, chew 1 targeted 3.5 Billion oral probiotic tablet (ProDentim). Slowly let the friendly bacteria populate your clean tooth enamel and gum pockets. Enjoy all-day fresh breath, whiter enamel, and naturally strong gums!
