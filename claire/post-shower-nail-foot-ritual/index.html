Step-by-Step Instructions

Claire's tested foolproof method for the ultimate soft texture.

Easy • 5 Steps
1 Step One

The Warm Shower Keratin Softening Window

Take your regular warm shower or soak your feet for 5 to 7 minutes in comfortably warm water. The natural steam and warmth gently soften the dense, hardened keratin layers of your toenails and callused heels, expanding microscopic pores that synthetic pharmacy creams simply cannot reach.

2 Step Two

Gentle Cotton Blotting (Preserving Keratin Hydration)

Step onto a clean bath mat and gently blot your toes and heels with a clean, dry cotton towel. Do not rub aggressively. The goal is to remove pooled surface water droplets while leaving the keratin cells saturated and plump, creating the ideal osmotic gradient for lipid-based oil absorption.

3 Step Three

Activating the 4-Oil Botanical Terpene Blend

Prepare your doctor-formulated organic oil blend containing organic tea tree, lavender, flaxseed, and sweet almond oils. These botanical terpenes target the fungal root network beneath the nail without triggering the drug-resistance mutations caused by synthetic pharmaceutical gels.

4 Step Four

Precision Dropper Application Along Nail Margins

Using the glass dropper, dispense 1 to 2 pure golden drops along the cuticle base, lateral nail grooves, and underneath the front tip of each affected nail. Natural capillary action immediately pulls the liquid blend deep into the subungual space where nail fungus hides.

5 Step Five

Micro-Circulation Massage & Breathable Protection

Take 20 seconds to massage any remaining oil into your toenails and rough heel cracks with gentle circular motions to stimulate peripheral blood flow. Slip on a pair of clean, breathable organic cotton socks for 30 minutes to seal in the botanicals and accelerate fresh, pink nail regrowth.
