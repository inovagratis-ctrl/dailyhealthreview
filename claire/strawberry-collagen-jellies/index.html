Step-by-Step Instructions

Claire's tested gentle-heat method for flawless, bouncy texture.

Easy • 5 Steps
1

Puree Fresh Strawberries & Lemon Juice

Wash, stem, and slice 2 cups (300g) of fresh organic strawberries. Add them to a blender along with 1 tablespoon (15ml) of freshly squeezed Meyer lemon juice (active Vitamin C is the biological cofactor needed for collagen crosslinking). Blend on high for 30 seconds until completely silky and ruby red.

Fresh organic strawberries, Meyer lemon & grass-fed gelatin

2

Bloom the Grass-Fed Gelatin

In a small stainless saucepan (off the heat), pour 1/2 cup of the cool strawberry puree. Evenly sprinkle 3 tablespoons (30g) of pure unflavored grass-fed gelatin across the surface. Let it rest undisturbed for 3 minutes to "bloom" until it swells and absorbs the fruit liquid into a thick gel.

Gently whisking strawberry puree & bloomed gelatin

3

Gentle Heat Dissolve (Keep It Below a Simmer)

Pour the remaining strawberry puree into the saucepan. Place over very low heat. Whisk gently and continuously for 2 to 3 minutes until all gelatin granules are completely liquefied and the mixture becomes glossy and clear. Claire's Rule: Never boil, as excessive heat can degrade delicate berry antioxidants.

Pouring glossy ruby liquid into flexible silicone bite molds

4

Chill in Refrigerator to Set

Transfer your filled silicone molds onto a flat baking tray and place in the refrigerator. Chill for 35 to 40 minutes until completely set, firm, and delightfully bouncy to the touch.

Popping firm, crystal-clear strawberry jellies onto ceramic plate

5

Pop, Savor & Reclaim Your Glow!

Gently push the bottom of the silicone cavities to pop the strawberry gems out. Enjoy 4 bouncy cubes in the morning or as an afternoon beauty snack. Store leftovers in an airtight glass jar in the fridge for up to 7 days!
