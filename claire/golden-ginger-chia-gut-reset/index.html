Step-by-Step Instructions

Claire's soothing mucilage blooming method.

Simple • 5 Steps
1

Brew Warm Ginger & Turmeric Tea

Steep 1 teaspoon of freshly grated ginger root and 1/4 teaspoon of turmeric powder in 1 cup (240ml) of hot water for 5 minutes. Strain into your favorite glass mug until comfortably warm (not scalding).

Brewing warm ginger & turmeric tea

2

Add Organic Chia Seeds

Add 2 tablespoons (24g) of whole black chia seeds into the warm tea. Use a spoon to give them an immediate thorough stir so they do not clump at the bottom.

Stirring in 2 tablespoons organic chia seeds

3

Squeeze Fresh Lemon & Optional Honey

Squeeze in the fresh juice from half a lemon (about 1 tbsp / 15ml). Add 1 teaspoon of raw honey if desired for natural taste and enzymes. Stir once more.

Squeezing fresh lemon juice & optional raw honey

4

Let Seeds Bloom for 10 Minutes

Allow the mixture to rest undisturbed for 10 minutes. As the chia seeds hydrate in the warm liquid, they expand up to 10 times their weight, forming a rich, silky mucilage gel that soothes the gut wall.

Blooming 10 minutes into a soothing prebiotic gel

5

Stir Gently & Drink First Thing in the Morning

Stir gently and drink warm on an empty stomach (or before your morning meal). Feel your stomach lighten and inflammation dissipate within 24 hours!
