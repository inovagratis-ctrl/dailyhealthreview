Step-by-Step Instructions

Claire's 30-second evening preparation method.

Super Fast • 5 Steps
1

Fill Your Glass with Ice

Choose an elegant stemmed crystal wine glass or coupe. Fill it to the brim with clear ice cubes so the mocktail stays crisp and chilled.

2

Pour Pure Tart Cherry Juice

Pour 1/2 cup (120ml) of 100% pure unsweetened Montmorency tart cherry juice directly over the ice. Watch the rich crimson color swirl through the cubes.

3

Stir in Magnesium Glycinate

Add 1 teaspoon of magnesium glycinate powder. Use a small cocktail stirrer or spoon to whisk gently for 10 seconds until completely dissolved without clumps.

4

Top with Sparkling Bubbles

Gently top with 1/2 cup (120ml) of chilled sparkling prebiotic water or plain seltzer. Enjoy the fizzy effervescence as it blends with the cherry juice.

5

Garnish with Rosemary & Sip 30 Min Before Bed

Gently clap a fresh rosemary sprig between your palms to release the calming pinene terpenes, then tuck it into the glass. Sip slowly 30 minutes before bed and drift off into restorative REM sleep!
