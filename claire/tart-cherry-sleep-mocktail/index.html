Step-by-Step Instructions

Claire's 30-second evening preparation method.

Super Fast • 5 Steps
1

Fill Your Glass with Ice

Choose an elegant stemmed crystal wine glass or stemless goblet. Fill it generously to the brim with clear ice cubes so the mocktail stays refreshing and ice-cold.

Filling glass with crisp ice cubes

2

Pour Pure Tart Cherry Juice

Pour 1/2 cup (120ml) of 100% pure unsweetened Montmorency tart cherry juice directly over the ice. Watch the rich, deep crimson red swirl gracefully through the cubes.

Pouring 100% pure Montmorency tart cherry juice

3

Stir in Magnesium Glycinate

Add 1 scoop (1 tsp / 2.5g) of pure magnesium glycinate powder. Use a small cocktail stirrer or spoon to whisk gently for 10 seconds until completely dissolved without clumps.

Whisking in calming magnesium glycinate

4

Top with Sparkling Prebiotic Bubbles

Gently top with 1/2 cup (120ml) of chilled sparkling prebiotic water or plain seltzer. Enjoy the fizzy effervescence as it blends with the cherry juice into a dreamy ruby cloud.

Topping with refreshing sparkling prebiotic bubbles

5

Garnish with Rosemary & Sip 30 Min Before Bed

Gently clap a fresh rosemary sprig between your palms to release calming pinene terpenes, then tuck it into the glass. Sip slowly 30 minutes before bed and drift off into uninterrupted REM sleep!
