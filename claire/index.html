1. 3-Ingredient Guilt-Free Chocolate Ice Cream

As seen in Claire's viral Instagram & TikTok video!

• 2 ripe bananas (sliced and frozen solid)

• 2 tbsp pure unsweetened raw cacao powder

• 2 tbsp unsweetened almond milk (or oat milk)

How to make: Blend everything in a high-speed blender for 60 seconds until silky smooth. Freeze for 30 minutes, scoop, and enjoy creamy chocolate heaven with zero dairy or refined sugar!

2. Cellular Turnover Green Smoothie

• 1 cup fresh baby spinach

• 1/2 ripe avocado (healthy lipids for skin barrier)

• 1 cup pure coconut water + squeeze of lemon

How to make: Blend with a couple ice cubes. Drink 30 minutes before breakfast to flood skin cells with bioavailable lutein and vitamin C cofactors.

3. Golden Turmeric & Ginger Bedtime Elixir

• 1 cup warm unsweetened almond milk

• 1/2 tsp ground turmeric + 1/4 tsp grated ginger

• Tiny pinch of black pepper (boosts curcumin absorption by 2,000%)

How to make: Whisk gently in a small saucepan over low heat. Sip warm 45 minutes before sleep to calm inflammation while cellular repair peaks.

🥣

4. Overnight Chia & Berry Collagen Pudding

• 2 tbsp chia seeds + 1 tbsp ground flaxseeds

• 3/4 cup unsweetened vanilla almond milk

• Handful of fresh wild blueberries

How to make: Stir in a small mason jar and refrigerate overnight. In the morning, top with blueberries for dense plant-based omega-3s that lock in hydration.
