The Underlying Biology of Chronic Nerve Hypersensitivity & Burning

Chronic nerve discomfort in the extremities—frequently presenting as burning sensations in the soles of the feet, pins-and-needles tingling, or stabbing nocturnal numbness—is primarily driven by sustained oxidative stress eroding the protective myelin sheath surrounding peripheral nerve fibers.

When local microcirculation to small nerve endings (vasa nervorum) becomes restricted, inflammatory metabolites accumulate, causing peripheral nociceptors to fire erratic pain signals to the spinal cord. NerveCalm intervenes at this biological root cause by neutralizing neuro-inflammatory compounds and replenishing essential botanical co-factors required for peripheral cellular repair.
