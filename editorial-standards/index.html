Our Editorial Standards & 5-Pillar Scoring Rubric

How Daily Health Review evaluates dietary supplements, grades clinical ingredient evidence, screens manufacturing safety, and enforces strict disqualification standards.

Why We Standardized Our Scoring System

The dietary supplement market in the United States generates over $50 billion annually, with tens of thousands of products competing for consumer attention. Unfortunately, marketing claims often overshadow biochemical reality. Many formulations rely on under-dosed "fairy dust" ingredients, undisclosed proprietary blends, or predatory recurring billing traps.

To protect consumers, Daily Health Review operates an objective, transparent, and reproducible 5-pillar evaluation framework. Every product reviewed on our portal receives an individual score from 0 to 10 across each pillar, resulting in a weighted composite score out of 10.0.

The 5-Pillar Evaluation Matrix

Our comprehensive weighted scoring rubric applied to every monograph.

1

Clinical Dose Concordance (Weight: 30%)

3.0 Max Points

We cross-reference active ingredients against published, peer-reviewed human clinical trials indexed in PubMed and the National Library of Medicine (NLM). Points are awarded based on whether the formula's dosage matches the therapeutic threshold proven effective in clinical research, rather than token quantities hidden inside generic blends.

Scoring Criteria: 9-10 (100% of actives meet or exceed human trial doses); 7-8 (Key primary active is fully dosed; secondary compounds are supportive); 5-6 (Partially under-dosed); <5 (Grossly under-dosed or reliant purely on in vitro data).
2

Manufacturing Standards & cGMP Verification (Weight: 25%)

2.5 Max Points

Formulas must be manufactured domestically in FDA-registered facilities operating strictly under Current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP, 21 CFR Part 111). We verify independent third-party laboratory testing for heavy metals (lead, arsenic, cadmium, mercury), microbiological contaminants (salmonella, E. coli), and ingredient identity.

Scoring Criteria: 9-10 (Verified cGMP facility + ISO 17025 accredited third-party Certificate of Analysis); 7-8 (cGMP certified with internal batch testing); <6 (Unclear domestic manufacturing origin or unverified testing).
3

Bioavailability & Molecular Form (Weight: 15%)

1.5 Max Points

Not all chemical forms are created equal. We evaluate whether a product utilizes superior bioavailable forms—such as chelated minerals (magnesium glycinate vs cheap oxide), phospholipid-bound phytosomes (Meriva® curcumin), standardized botanical extracts (Bergavit®, Q-Actin™), and enteric protections for live probiotic strains.

Scoring Criteria: 9-10 (Patented or premium chelated/phytosome delivery); 7-8 (Standardized botanical extracts); <6 (Cheap inorganic mineral salts or raw unstandardized plant powders).
4

Label Transparency & Blend Integrity (Weight: 15%)

1.5 Max Points

We demand full disclosure. Supplements that disclose exact milligram amounts for every active botanical receive top marks. Brands that hide active ingredients behind opaque proprietary blends to obscure cheap filler content are heavily penalized.

Scoring Criteria: 9-10 (100% transparent supplement facts panel with exact milligrams); 7-8 (Semi-transparent proprietary blend with primary active disclosed); <5 (Opaque proprietary blend hiding active quantities).
5

Guarantee Terms & Fair Billing (Weight: 15%)

1.5 Max Points

Consumer safety includes financial safety. We audit manufacturer refund policies to verify a minimum 60 to 180-day money-back guarantee, absence of forced recurring subscriptions, clear customer support response times, and straightforward refund processing.

Scoring Criteria: 9-10 (60 to 180-day unconditional refund policy, one-time checkout, responsive support); 7-8 (60-day standard refund policy); <5 (Short 30-day window, hidden restocking fees, or difficult refund pathways).
Automatic Disqualification & Rejection Standards

A product is immediately disqualified and excluded from Daily Health Review's top recommendations if it violates any of the following consumer safety rules:

  • ✕ Unlawful Disease Claims: Any marketing asserting that a dietary supplement can cure, treat, reverse, or prevent diagnosed chronic illnesses (violating FDA 21 CFR §101.93).
  • ✕ Undisclosed Pharmaceuticals: Contamination or inclusion of synthetic pharmaceutical analogs, unlisted stimulants, or banned athletic compounds.
  • ✕ Predatory Auto-Ship Traps: Enrolling customers into unannounced monthly recurring billing without conspicuous, affirmative consent.
  • ✕ Refusal of Laboratory Traceability: Failure to provide proof of cGMP facility registration or verified batch testing.

Corrections, Inquiries & Manufacturer Updates

Daily Health Review is committed to absolute accuracy. If a manufacturer updates their formula, provides newly published human clinical trials, or alters packaging details, our research desk reviews submitted documentation promptly.

Correction Guarantee: Verified factual corrections or formula updates are reviewed and investigated within 48 business hours.

Submissions & Clinical Documentation: Send peer-reviewed study links or updated COAs directly to our research desk at editorial@dailyhealthreviewus.com.
