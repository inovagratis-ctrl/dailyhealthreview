Editorial Standards & Consumer Mission

Empowering Consumers Through Evidence-Based Wellness Guidance

Daily Health Review is an independent digital health publication and consumer research desk dedicated to deconstructing dietary supplements, evaluating published clinical evidence, and helping you make informed decisions.

Our Core Mission

The dietary supplement market in the United States contains tens of thousands of products, often accompanied by complex labels, proprietary blends, and confusing marketing claims. Our goal is straightforward: cut through the noise, audit formulas against peer-reviewed scientific studies, and provide clear, transparent ratings based on real clinical merit.

We do not manufacture or distribute supplements. Instead, we operate as a dedicated consumer research portal, analyzing ingredient quality, manufacturer safety certifications, refund guarantees, and real user experiences across major dietary categories.

Methodology & Scoring System

How We Calculate Our Product Scores

Every supplement featured on our portal is evaluated across four weighted pillars before receiving an overall rating out of 10.

1 Weight: 30%

Clinical Ingredient Evidence

We cross-reference active ingredients with published trials in recognized medical journals (e.g. PubMed, American Journal of Clinical Nutrition). Formulas must use standardized extracts and effective physiological dosages.

2 Weight: 30%

cGMP & Third-Party Lab Safety

Products must be manufactured in FDA-registered, cGMP-certified facilities in the United States. We verify that brands perform third-party testing to ensure zero microbial contamination, heavy metals, or undisclosed fillers.

3 Weight: 20%

Money-Back Guarantee Terms

We only recommend products backed by a minimum 60 to 180-day 100% money-back guarantee. Consumer protection is essential, and readers must have adequate time to test formulations without financial risk.

4 Weight: 20%

Cost Per Daily Serving & Value

We calculate the daily cost of a full recommended dose against competing market alternatives. Multi-bottle discounts, free shipping options, and official manufacturer promotions heavily factor into value ratings.

Editorial Independence & Affiliate Compensation

Transparency is central to everything we do. Daily Health Review participates in affiliate marketing programs, including ClickBank, Digistore24, and direct manufacturer partner programs. When you click on links on our site and make a purchase, we may receive a referral commission at zero additional cost to you.

This affiliate compensation supports our research operations, web hosting, and independent writing team. However, commercial partnerships never dictate our editorial verdict. Products that do not meet our quality and safety criteria are excluded from our top recommendations, regardless of commission rates.

For more detailed legal information, please consult our dedicated Affiliate Disclosure and FDA & Medical Disclaimer.
