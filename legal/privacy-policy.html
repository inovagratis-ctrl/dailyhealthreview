Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Vitality Health Reports (accessible via vitalityhealthreports.vercel.app), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our website, we may collect non-personally identifiable information automatically, such as browser type, operating system, referring URLs, device information, and pages visited. If you contact us directly or subscribe to our newsletter, we may receive personal information such as your name and email address.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and editorial publications.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand website speed and user experience.
  • Understand and analyze how visitors interact with our content.
  • Send periodic health tips, report updates, and relevant communications (only if subscribed).
  • Prevent fraudulent activities and ensure security.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Like any other modern website, Vitality Health Reports uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and other information.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Affiliate Partners

Third-party ad servers or affiliate networks (such as ClickBank, Google Analytics, or advertising platforms) use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective links and advertisements. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Vitality Health Reports has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

5. CCPA & GDPR Privacy Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users have the right to request access to, deletion of, or restriction of their personal data. If you wish to make a request, please contact us.
