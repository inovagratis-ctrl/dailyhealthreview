Biography & Editorial Philosophy

Sarah Lin holds a Master of Science in Nutritional Biochemistry and has spent over a decade analyzing consumer healthcare products, functional foods, and standardized herbal supplements. Prior to joining Daily Health Review, Sarah worked in independent third-party laboratory verification, examining Certificate of Analysis (COA) documents and screening botanical raw materials for adulteration, pesticide residues, and heavy metal compliance.

At Daily Health Review, Sarah leads our formula deconstruction team. Her methodology centers on cutting through aggressive marketing jargon to analyze what is genuinely inside each capsule, drop, or powder. She cross-references proprietary blends against human clinical trials published in PubMed, verifying whether a manufacturer’s active compounds match the therapeutic dosages established in peer-reviewed scientific literature.

Core Verification Standards Enforced by Sarah:

  • ✓ Dose-Response Concordance: Screening that active botanicals match clinically validated human study levels, not merely token "fairy dust" amounts.
  • ✓ Manufacturing Traceability: Ensuring domestic production occurs strictly in FDA-registered, cGMP-certified American facilities.
  • ✓ Consumer Advocacy & Fair Billing: Auditing guarantee policies to confirm 60 to 180-day unconditional refund processing without hidden subscription traps.

Professional Ethics & Independence Statement

Sarah Lin accepts zero gifts, free product samples for biased coverage, or direct consultation retainers from any dietary supplement manufacturer.
