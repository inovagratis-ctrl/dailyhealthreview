Clinical Background & Medical Governance Role

Marcus Vance, MD, is a board-certified physician with extensive post-graduate focus on clinical pharmacology, metabolic biochemistry, and integrative preventative medicine. Throughout his career in outpatient clinical practice and medical research, Dr. Vance has evaluated the biological mechanisms through which dietary nutrients, botanical extracts, and bioactive lipids interact with human physiology.

At Daily Health Review, Dr. Vance provides independent clinical governance for educational health guides, micronutrient monographs, and high-complexity health protocols. His role is dedicated to patient safety and scientific rigor: verifying that all biochemical claims are supported by peer-reviewed human clinical trials, identifying potential prescription medication interactions, and ensuring that readers receive realistic, evidence-grounded health information.

Clinical Standards Enforced by Dr. Vance:

  • ✓ Elemental Dose Precision: Ensuring all discussions of mineral compounds (e.g., magnesium glycinate, zinc picolinate) distinguish between total compound mass and actual bioavailable elemental yield.
  • ✓ Drug-Nutrient Contraindication Screening: Auditing guides to mandate explicit warnings regarding blood thinners, antihypertensives, antibiotics, and renal impairment.
  • ✓ Human Evidence Threshold: Differentiating preliminary in vitro / rodent findings from rigorous randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled human trials.

Clinical Scope & Medical Disclaimer

Dr. Marcus Vance provides editorial and scientific consultation to Daily Health Review solely in a medical advisory capacity. His review of educational content does not constitute a doctor-patient relationship, clinical diagnosis, or personalized medical prescription. Readers must always seek direct guidance from their own licensed physician before adopting new dietary supplements, altering prescribed regimens, or initiating therapeutic interventions.
