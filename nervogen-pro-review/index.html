1. What is NervoGen Pro™ & How Does It Work?

NervoGen Pro™ is an over-the-counter botanical dietary supplement formulated to support peripheral nerve wellness, soothe tingling and burning sensory discomfort in the extremities, and promote relaxed microvascular blood flow to distal nerve endings.

Peripheral nerve endings in the hands and feet are sensitive to microvascular blood flow reductions, persistent low-grade oxidative stress, and inflammatory cytokine activity. Over time, these insults can trigger hyperexcitable nerve signaling, resulting in numbness, prickling, or sensory hypersensitivity. NervoGen Pro targets peripheral nerve comfort through three coordinated biochemical actions:

1

Signal Modulation

Corydalis and California Poppy contain bioactive alkaloids that modulate spinal nociceptive signal transmission, calming hyperactive sensory sensations.

2

GABA-Ergic Calm

Passiflora Incarnata delivers natural chrysin flavonoids that interact with central GABA receptors, helping down-regulate systemic sensory stress and night-time agitation.

3

Oxidative Shield

Prickly Pear betalains and Marshmallow root polysaccharides provide antioxidant and mucosal-soothing defenses against free-radical damage along peripheral nerve sheaths.

2. Botanical Ingredient Analysis: What Does Clinical Literature Show?

Our medical board analyzed each of the 5 active botanical components in the NervoGen Pro formula against independent peer-reviewed pharmacology databases:

Corydalis Yanhusuo (Standardized Dehydrocorybulbine / DHCB)

Sensory Signaling Attenuation

Corydalis has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries. Modern clinical investigations identified dehydrocorybulbine (DHCB) as its active constituent, which attenuates inflammatory and neuropathic sensory impulses without generating tolerance or dependence (Zhang et al., Curr Biol, PMID: 24520148).

Passiflora Incarnata (Passionflower)

GABA Neuro-Calming Modulation

Passiflora Incarnata contains bioflavonoids such as apigenin and chrysin that bind to GABA-A receptors, calming neural agitation, attenuating stress-induced nerve firing, and facilitating deep restorative sleep (Appel et al., Phytother Res, PMID: 21197646).

California Poppy Seeds (Eschscholzia californica)

Peripheral Sensory Relief

California Poppy is a non-narcotic botanical containing protopine alkaloids that exert gentle neuro-sedative, muscle-relaxing, and sensory-soothing properties in distal sensory nerves (Rolland et al., Planta Med, PMID: 1780302).

Prickly Pear (Opuntia ficus-indica)

Microvascular Antioxidant Shield

Prickly pear fruit contains high concentrations of indicaxanthin and betanin, bioavailable antioxidants proven in human trials to neutralize lipid peroxidation and defend microvascular capillary integrity (Tesoriere et al., Am J Clin Nutr, PMID: 15277160).

Marshmallow Root (Althaea officinalis)

Cellular Soothing Mucilage

Marshmallow root extract provides high-molecular-weight rhamnogalacturonan polysaccharides that exhibit anti-inflammatory and tissue-repair effects on cell membranes, promoting hydration and tissue comfort (Deters et al., J Ethnopharmacol, PMID: 19799989).
