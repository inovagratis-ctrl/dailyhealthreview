Why Do Sudden Bladder Urges & Accidental Leaks Happen?

For decades, women experiencing sudden urgency or leaking while laughing, sneezing, or exercising were told it was simply an unavoidable sign of aging or weakened pelvic muscles. While pelvic floor tone is important, recent urological research reveals a previously overlooked biological factor: the urinary microbiome.

Just like the gut, the bladder and urinary tract possess a delicate ecosystem of protective microflora. When this ecosystem becomes depleted due to hormonal fluctuations, dietary stressors, or environmental toxins, undesirable bacteria trigger micro-irritations in the bladder lining. This chronic irritation sends false "full" signals to the brain, sparking involuntary muscle spasms and sudden, uncontrollable leaks.
