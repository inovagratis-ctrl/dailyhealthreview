Editorial Policy & Review Standards

How Daily Health Review investigates, fact-checks, and audits nutritional supplements, longevity protocols, and botanical formulations.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Review Board Desk

1. Our Core Editorial Mission

At Daily Health Review, our primary responsibility is to the reader. In an era where the dietary supplement industry is crowded with unsubstantiated marketing claims, proprietary filler blends, and misleading influencer endorsements, we provide independent, scientifically grounded analysis.

Our editorial desk operates with complete separation between our journalistic investigations and commercial monetization channels. Every article, recipe, and ingredient breakdown is authored with the explicit goal of translating complex clinical biochemistry into clear, actionable, and verified guidance.

2. The 5-Pillar Supplement Auditing Framework

Before any dietary supplement or nutritional formulation is evaluated or referenced on our platform, it must undergo our standardized 5-pillar examination:

Pillar 1: Clinical Dosage Integrity

We verify whether individual ingredients match the exact milligram thresholds proven effective in double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled human clinical trials, strictly penalizing "fairy dust" under-dosages.

Pillar 2: Bioavailability & Molecular Form

We evaluate the exact chemical form of nutrients (e.g., chelated magnesium bisglycinate vs cheap magnesium oxide; re-esterified triglycerides vs synthetic ethyl esters), prioritizing cellular uptake.

Pillar 3: cGMP & Third-Party Testing

We inspect manufacturing protocols, demanding compliance with NSF/USP cGMP guidelines, ISO-17025 accredited third-party laboratory verification for heavy metals, microbial contaminants, and label purity.

Pillar 4: Transparency & Full Label Disclosure

We strongly favor brands with 100% transparent Supplement Facts panels, penalizing hidden proprietary complexes that obscure actual active ingredient concentrations.

Pillar 5: Consumer Protection & Money-Back Guarantees

We verify manufacturer terms, ensuring full refund compliance through reputable merchant processors (such as ClickBank and Digistore24) with minimum 60-day trial windows and zero hidden auto-rebill traps.

3. Hierarchy of Scientific Evidence

Our editors and medical contributors adhere to the evidence-based pyramid recognized by the global medical community. When citing research, we prioritize:

  • Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses published in high-impact medical journals (e.g., The Lancet, JAMA, New England Journal of Medicine, Cochrane Library).
  • Double-Blind, Randomized, Placebo-Controlled Human Trials (RCTs) indexed on the National Institutes of Health (PubMed/NCBI).
  • Monographs and Reference Guidelines from authoritative bodies including the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA), World Health Organization (WHO), and the National Institutes of Health Office of Dietary Supplements (ODS).
  • We explicitly exclude: Non-peer-reviewed white papers funded exclusively by proprietary ingredient vendors without independent validation, animal-only in-vitro models presented as human proof, and anecdotal testimonials.

4. Editorial Independence & Commercial Disclosures

Daily Health Review is supported in part through affiliate partnerships and programmatic advertising. However, we maintain rigid safeguards:

  • No Pay-to-Rank: Manufacturers cannot purchase positive editorial reviews, higher rankings, or clinical endorsements.
  • Unbiased Critique: If a formulation has questionable dosing, excessive excipients, or artificial food dyes, we disclose it unequivocally regardless of commercial relationships.
  • Clear FTC Disclosures: In compliance with Federal Trade Commission (16 CFR Part 255) guidelines, every page containing commercial links features an unambiguous disclosure before any promotional material.

5. Fact-Checking, Updates & Corrections Policy

Nutritional science is dynamic. When new clinical trials contradict previous findings or when supplement manufacturers alter formulation panels, we actively update our articles.

If a reader, clinical researcher, or manufacturer detects a factual inaccuracy in our reporting, we encourage prompt notification. Our editorial board investigates all inquiries within 48 business hours and publishes transparent correction notices when revisions occur.

Editorial Inquiries Desk: Direct clinical corrections or evidence submissions to editorial@dailyhealthreviewus.com.
