Suggested Use & Daily Dropper Routine

Official Label Directions: Adults, take 1 mL (approximately 2 droppers full) twice daily or as directed by a healthcare professional. Each 2 Fl Oz bottle contains 60 servings (a full 30-day supply at two servings per day).

Morning Routine (1 mL Serving)

Many users follow the brand's sublingual routine in the morning before coffee or breakfast, holding the measured liquid briefly under the tongue before swallowing.

Evening Routine & Sensitivities

Take the second 1 mL dose as directed. Because the blend includes Green Tea leaf and Guarana seed extracts, individuals sensitive to caffeine may prefer taking the second serving earlier in the afternoon rather than right at bedtime.
