The Root Cause of Aging Skin: Understanding "Skin Cell Turnover"

Have you ever wondered why a small blemish or scratch in a 20-year-old vanishes in mere days, while in your 40s or 50s, dullness, fine lines, and stubborn dark spots linger for months?

The biological explanation is Cell Turnover. This is your skin’s natural rejuvenation cycle: shedding accumulated dead surface cells and replacing them with fresh, resilient, collagen-rich young cells. When turnover is rapid, skin naturally looks radiant, pores remain unclogged, and fine lines smooth out.
